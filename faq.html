We manage and operate ships for owners engaged in the transportation of goods, with a focus on vessels used in commodity trading. That covers technical management, crew management, OPEX management and fleet management, together with arranging sea transport for the cargo those vessels carry.

Four core services: technical management (maintenance, repairs and regulatory compliance), crew management (recruiting, training and managing seafarers), OPEX management (monitoring and reducing vessel operating costs) and fleet management (asset management and fleet development). Each is described on its own page under Services.

Crude oil, refined petroleum products, chemicals and liquefied gases as liquid cargo, and bulk cargo such as grains, edible oils, iron ore, coal, minerals, cement and aggregates. We also arrange international sea transport as part of wider logistics chains.

Through our network of manning offices and training centres. Crews are recruited, trained and managed to meet the staffing needs of each vessel, with an emphasis on competence, safety and retention.

Our technical and marine teams are responsible for making sure each vessel meets all applicable international and local maritime regulations, from routine inspections and planned maintenance to class and flag requirements.

By monitoring vessel spending continuously and managing it proactively. The aim is to maximise vessel performance while reducing operational expenditure, without compromising safety or compliance.
