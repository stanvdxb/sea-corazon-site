About Sea Corazon Ship Management FZCO

We manage and operate vessels engaged in the transportation of goods: technical ship management, crew management, OPEX management and auxiliary ship services. Our commitment is to offer dependable, economical, and convenient solutions for our clients’ maritime logistics requirements in commodity trading. Our focus is on prioritizing safety, efficiency, and compliance with regulations to enhance vessel performance significantly. By optimizing our clients’ supply chain operations, we aim to alleviate them from the operational challenges they may encounter.
