Crew Management

Embark on our Ship Crew Management services at Sea Corazon Ship Management. We understand that the heart of maritime operations lies in the skilled and dedicated hands of the crew. Our crew management services are meticulously designed to alleviate the operational burden on ship owners, ensuring the recruitment, training, and management of highly qualified seafarers. We plan crew rotations and manage onboard performance, so your vessels stay manned with qualified seafarers.

Our global network of manning offices and training centers lets us source, train, and manage a pool of competent seafarers. Our crew management services encompass crew planning, recruitment, and training, ensuring that your vessels are manned with qualified professionals who meet the highest industry standards. With a focus on compliance, safety, and crew welfare, our crew management services not only enhance the operational efficiency of your fleet but also cultivate a positive and productive onboard environment. We handle crew management and staffing for your vessels.
